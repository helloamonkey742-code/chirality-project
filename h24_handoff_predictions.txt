h24_handoff_predictions.txt  (written 2026-10-21, before any result-producing run; frozen once hashed)

Unit: h24_handoff.  Question: can a handoff rule predict the final hand of the radial runs in >= 95 % of runs out of
sample (H24, journals/ija_draft.md near line 282; E58 at line 158), and what should happen to the world-wrapper draws
where no layer up to the ice is thick enough?

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WAS ALREADY SEEN (so these are not predictions)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The 600 old runs (deephand_scale.log, seed 20261012) were read in full before this file was written, including in
  scratchpad exploratory fits (logistic fits of "rule domain holds" on d/w, drift, neighbour d/w; cross-validation on
  the old runs). The three variants below were chosen and fitted on those runs.
  The old-run numbers (in sample and 10-fold x 5 CV) are therefore development numbers, not tests.
- Development run (disclosed): `python h24_handoff.py fit` printed coefficients and old-run agreements. From it:

  = [-1.76, 7.33, 1.68, -1.98]; ln eps clip [-8.38, -0.46], ln d/w-above clip [0.15, 4.35]; 235 training rows.
  Old runs in sample: plain 81.0, THR=1 93.3, THR=3 76.8, V1 95.2, V2 96.8, V3 98.2 %.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE RULES UNDER TEST (frozen; all fitted on the old 600 runs only)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Domains = maximal runs of equal hand in the stacked layers. The rule domain is the one containing r* (deepest-hand
rule). Handoff: if the rule domain does not hold, the deciding role passes to the domain above, and so on.
  d/w     = domain thickness / local wall width, at the domain middle (handoff.validate's quantity).
  eps     = 2 w_loc(top) (x_top - 1) / r_top, x_top = r_top / r* (dimensionless drift of the domain's rising top wall).
  dab     = d/w of the domain above.
  THR     : holds iff d/w >= THR (handoff.py's rule; THR = 1 and THR = 3 are the published ones). Exhausted chain ->
            no hand (counted as a miss).
  V1      : the top domain holds iff d/w >= 1.25; every other domain holds iff d/w >= 1.25.
  V2      : domain (not the top one) holds iff d/w >= 3 or z > 0, z = -2.67 + 5.65 d/w + 1.50 ln eps.
  V3      : as V2 with z = -1.76 + 7.33 d/w + 1.68 ln eps - 1.98 ln dab.
  In V2 and V3 the top domain holds iff d/w >= 1.25. ln eps and ln dab are clipped to the training range above.
  Chain exhausted (no domain holds): fallback 'deep' = the rule domain's own hand (default, fixed now); the
  alternatives 'top' (the top domain's hand) and 'none' are reported as secondary.
Decision rule for the primary variant: the variant (V1, V2, V3) with the highest old-run CV overall agreement; ties
within 0.2 points go to the one with fewer parameters. Chosen before testing; it uses only old runs.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE TESTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Fresh runs (F): deephand_scale.one with seed 20261021: w = 0.035, 0.02, 0.01, 0.005 with 150, 150, 150, 120 runs
  (570); w = 0.0025 is skipped for cost (~10 min per run). Configurations drawn as in deephand_scale.config.
Thin stacks (T): n = 10 layers, w = 0.10 and 0.07, 60 runs each (120), same seed family; they probe the regime
  where every domain is thinner than a wall width (zero old runs).
Noise reruns (N): every THR=1 miss of the old runs plus every old run with a non-top rule domain of 0.5 <= d/w < 3 at
  w >= 0.005, same configuration, two fresh noise seeds each (177 configurations, 354 reruns).
Agreement = predicted final hand == observed final hand (fin) from the simulation; 'fired' = the chosen domain is
  not the rule domain. SE = binomial.

PRIMARY
P1  The primary variant (decision rule above) on the 570 fresh runs: overall agreement >= 95.0 % AND agreement on
    fired runs >= 85.0 %.  PASS = both hold (the pre-registered target of handoff.py's docstring, point estimates).
    FAIL otherwise; then H24 stays open and the report states the measured agreement with its SE and which
    criterion failed.
P2  The decision rule picks V3.  PASS if V3; FAIL otherwise (a record; it is a deterministic function of the old-run
    CV numbers 95.2 / 96.4 / 97.7, so FAIL is not expected).

SECONDARY (fresh runs, fallback 'deep')
P3  V3 overall agreement >= V1 overall agreement + 1.0 point.
P4  V1 overall agreement >= THR=1 (chain exhausted -> no hand) overall agreement + 0.5 point.
P5  THR=3 (chain exhausted -> no hand) overall agreement < 85.0 %.  (The old runs give 76.8 %; the old-run w mix
    gives about 76 % on the fresh w mix.)
P6  A d/w-only rule has a ceiling: the best single d/w threshold chosen ON the fresh runs (an oracle, fallback 'deep',
    grid 0 to 5 step 0.05) gives overall agreement <= 96.0 %, and the primary variant exceeds that oracle by
    >= 0.5 point.  PASS = both.
P7  Stratified: the primary variant has overall agreement >= 90.0 % in every w stratum (4) and every n stratum
    (3, 6, 10) of the fresh runs.
P8  Noise is not the ceiling: of the reruns of THR=1-miss configurations (40 configurations x 2), >= 90 % reproduce
    the original run's final hand; of the reruns of the borderline configurations (137 x 2), >= 85 % do.
    PASS = both.  (FAIL would mean part of the ~7 % residual is seed noise, not configuration.)

THIN REGIME (thin stacks + old + fresh runs pooled; group G = runs whose every domain from the top down to the rule
domain has d/w < 1)
P9  G has at least 20 runs.  PASS if n(G) >= 20; if not, P10 and P11 are reported as NOT TESTABLE.
P10 In G, the fraction whose final hand equals the top domain's hand exceeds the fraction whose final hand equals the
    rule domain's hand by >= 10 points (prior: the chain keeps passing the role upward).
P11 In G, neither fixed fallback ('deep', 'top') reaches 85 % (prior: the regime cannot be closed with a fixed
    fallback). PASS if both are below 85 %.

WORLDS (h24_handoff_worlds.py; N = 200000, seeds 0-3, fitted h as in handoff.py; B = primary variant, fallback 'deep')
W1  Regression: the script's copy of handoff.hand reproduces handoff.hand run for run (arrays equal) for handoff off,
    THR = 3 and THR = 1 in all 15 worlds (seed 0, no lid), and handoff.log's printed THR = 3 and THR = 1 columns.
    PASS = exact.
W2  Monotone-chain shortcut: the deciding layer is found by bisection on the layer index. Against an exact walk on the
    first 1500 recursion draws of each of the 15 worlds (seed 0, no lid), mismatches <= 0.5 % of draws for V2 and
    for V3.  PASS = both.
W3  Early Earth ocean P_pved (final, seed 0) under B/deep lies in [97.0, 99.9] %.
W4  The movement of the no-layer draws: M = P_pved(B, deep) - P_pved(B, none) lies in [0, 10] points in every one of
    the 15 worlds, no lid and final; Early Earth M <= 2.5.  PASS = all.
W5  Rank stability: Spearman correlation across the 15 worlds (no lid, seed 0) between P_pved under B/deep and under
    THR = 1 is >= 0.98.
W6  Seed span: max - min of P_pved over seeds 0-3 for B/deep is <= 0.5 point in every world (no lid and final).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DECISION RULE FOR WHAT GETS REPORTED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- P1 PASS: H24 (radial agreement >= 95 %) is reported as validated out of sample for w >= 0.005 and for the wrapper
  geometries sampled (R/H 2-100, n = 3-10 layers), with the variant's SE. Scope limits stated: no w = 0.0025 runs, no
  runs where all domains are thin unless P9 passes, 1-D radial model only.
- P1 FAIL: H24 is reported as narrowed: the measured fresh agreement of every variant, the residual misses by type,
  and why (thickness / drift / neighbour / noise) from P6-P8. The 93 % ceiling claim is then restated with the
  numbers of this run, not with the old-run in-sample numbers.
- No threshold, variant, coefficient or fallback is changed after the results run. If course must change, a v2
  predictions file is written and the change is stated plainly in the notes.
- Failed predictions are reported as failed, in the same table as the ones that held.
