QUESTION
  uncertainty.py:36-39 says Ashkenazy & Tziperman 2021 "use 30" as a horizontal D_h and brackets the icy-moon D_h at
  1e-2..1e2 m2/s. I read papers/h14_eddy/ashkenazy2021.txt myself (python3 -I, 2026-10-21): no "30" as a diffusivity;
  explicit horizontal tracer diffusion 5 (2D) and 2 (3D) m2/s at lines 402-405; diagnosed eddy diffusion/viscosity about
  300 m2/s and a rough lower bound about 200 m2/s for the global mean at lines 553-559. So the diagnosed value is ABOVE
  the top of the bracket (1e2). How far do the paper's per-world shares move if the upper bound goes to 3e2 and 1e3?

DESIGN (fixed here)
  Same code path as multiwall.log Section 4: multiwall.run4(seed, full=True), N = 200000, seed 0, common random numbers.
  uncertainty.logu(lo, hi) uses the same uniform stream for any range, so a draw with uniform u has
  log10 D_h = -2 + u*(4 + d), d = log10(hi/1e2) = 0, 0.477, 1.0. Only D_h moves; every other input, the salt-lid mask
  and the extra draws (eta, r, rho) are bit-identical across hi.
  Range override: U.WORLDS["Enceladus"] and U.WORLDS["Europa"] (D_h entry) and ocean_worlds.DH (the 12 other moons).
  Early Earth keeps 1e2..1e4 (its own entry), so it must not move at all.
  Metric: the paper's headline = multiwall P_pved ("success"), main rule, printed as "no lid / final"; also cascade x10
  (the low-end timing sensitivity), P_chance and P_none. Enceladus is run twice: with the seed collision as logged
  (reproduces multiwall.log) and with the seedfix.py wrapper (the paper's Enceladus; 26.4, seedfix.log:122,125).
  Seeds 1-3 are run with the fix on, for the stability of the changes.
  Icy worlds = the 14 non-Earth worlds. M0 = main P_pved no lid, MF = main P_pved final (after the salt lid),
  X0/XF = the same at cascade x10, C0 = main P_chance no lid.

CALIBRATION (from existing logs only; no dh_range run before this file)
  rerun_base.log:106,120,134,148 give P_pved in 10 equal-count D_h bins (centres log10 D_h = -1.8 ... +1.8) for
  Enceladus main/x10 and Europa main/x10. Because the other inputs are independent of u, the expected change from the
  rescaling is exactly the integral over u of p(l_new(u)) - p(l_old(u)) with p the binned curve; above 1e2 I extrapolate
  linearly with the slope of bins 6-10. Checked on the old range: the integral gives 28.2, 19.8, 39.6, 29.0 against the
  logged 28.3, 19.9, 39.6, 29.0. Result (points, no lid):
                      3e2     1e3
     Enceladus main  -0.42   -0.88
     Enceladus x10   -0.34   -0.70
     Europa main     -0.24   -0.53
     Europa x10      -0.25   -0.50
  No D_h decile curve exists for the other 12 moons. I scale their change to their own level: -1.0 % of the 1e2 level
  at 3e2 and -2.2 % at 1e3 (the mean of Enceladus -1.5 / -3.1 % and Europa -0.6 / -1.3 %).
  Mechanism: the sideways coarsening time (L/5.5)^2/D_h, at the smallest D_h = 1e-2, is at most 7 % of the world's
  shortest age (Ganymede 7.2 Myr against 100 Myr; Enceladus 5.6e4 yr against 1 Myr; computed by hand 2026-10-21, not a
  log line), so "heals across" is always true and a larger D_h only thins the deciding layer: d_w = L sqrt(D_z/D_h)
  falls as D_h^(-1/2), the share f falls with it, and Delta ~ sqrt(f) falls as D_h^(-1/4), so fewer draws reach
  F*Delta >= 2.

  P2  Early Earth is unchanged by the bound: every printed number equals its hi = 1e2 value, exactly.
  P3  DIRECTION. M0 falls as the bound rises in the icy worlds: M0(1e3) < M0(1e2) by at least 0.1 in at least 13 of
      the 14, and M0(1e3) <= M0(3e2) <= M0(1e2) in at least 13 of the 14 (no sampling noise on a CRN difference of
      that size: about 0.02 points).
  P4  SIZE. (a) Every icy world: M0 and X0 change between -1.0 and 0.0 at 3e2 and between -2.0 and 0.0 at 1e3.
      (b) Enceladus (fixed) and Europa M0 changes within +-0.4 of the central values at 3e2 and +-0.6 at 1e3
      (Enceladus -0.4 / -0.9, Europa -0.2 / -0.5).
  P5  LID. Enceladus MF = 0.0 at every hi. For Europa, Dione, Miranda, Mimas: the MF change has the same sign as
      the M0 change and is no larger in size.
  P6  ORDER. Rank the 15 worlds by MF at each hi (Enceladus fixed). At 1e2 the closest neighbours are Oberon/Titania
      (gap 0.1), Ganymede/Callisto (0.6), Titan/Triton (1.2), Titania/Ariel (1.7). The order changes at most among
      pairs whose 1e2 gap is 0.7 or less (Oberon/Titania, Ganymede/Callisto). Any change in a pair with a 1e2 gap of
      1.2 or more FAILS this prediction. The block order (Earth, then Umbriel/Oberon/Titania/Ariel, then Ganymede/
      Callisto/Pluto, then Triton/Titan, then Europa, Dione, Miranda, Mimas, Enceladus last) is kept.
  P7  WHERE THE MASS GOES. P_chance (C0) rises by at least 0.05 at 1e3 in at least 12 of the 14 icy worlds, and the
      one-hand share M0 + C0 changes by at most 0.5 points in size at 1e3 in every icy world (the loss in P_pved is
      mostly moved to P_chance, because a thinner deciding layer makes the draw weaker, not later). This is the
      weakest prediction: no log has P_chance against D_h.
  P8  DOSE-RESPONSE. For every icy world with M0 change <= -0.3 at 3e2, change(1e3) / change(3e2) lies in 1.7 to 2.6
      (log10 of the bound ratio is 1.0 / 0.477 = 2.1 and the response is near-linear in log10 D_h). Scored on those
      worlds; at least 80 % must pass; fewer than 3 qualifying worlds means not scorable.
  P9  STABILITY. At seeds 1, 2, 3 the M0 change at 1e3 differs from the seed 0 change by at most 0.3 in every
      icy world.

DECISION RULES (set before the run)
  - P3, P4a and P6 hold: the D_h upper bound is not what the headline shares depend on. The comment at
    uncertainty.py:38-39 is wrong about the source but the numbers do not move by more than about 2 points; the fix is
    wording, plus one sentence in the paper that says the bracket is assumed and what 3e2 and 1e3 do.
  - Any icy world with a change below -3 at 1e3, or any order change in a pair with a 1e2 gap of 1.2 or more: the
    shares depend on the assumed bracket and the paper needs the 3e2 / 1e3 rows in its sensitivity section.
  - A failed prediction is reported as failed in journals/new_analyses_dh_range.md, with the number.
