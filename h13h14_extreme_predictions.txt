QUESTION. The sideways-coarsening slowdown f (U.A = 5.5/sqrt(f), so the sweep's sideways time (L/A)^2/D_h is multiplied
by f in every draw) was bounded in h13h14_combined.log up to f = 1000 (Ganymede loses 11.90 points, Early Earth 0.0025).
h14c found the eddy slowdown S50 at l/w = 10 grows like (D_eff/D_mol)^0.40, so f = 17 x S could extrapolate to about
1e5 - 1e6.  What does each world's share, and its rank, become for f far beyond 1000, up to f -> infinity, and does Early
Earth's 99.9 survive?  Method: h13h14_extreme.py (a copy of the h13h14_combined.py wrapper, same script h13_sphere_shares.py
unchanged) at f = 1, 1000, 3000, 1e4, 3e4, 1e5, 3e5, 1e6, 3e6, 1e7, 1e8, 1e9, 1e12.  Loss = share at f = 1 minus share at
f, in points of all N = 200,000 draws, seed 0.  Columns: main no lid, main final, x10 no lid, x10 final ("main columns" =
the first two; they are the scored ones unless a line says otherwise).  "Largest loss" = the line the script prints
(max over 15 worlds and the 4 columns; the first column on a tie).  Count of worlds losing >= T = how many of the 15 worlds
have loss >= T in that column.  Rank = 1 + the number of worlds whose unrounded share is STRICTLY greater (shares compared
after round(x, 9)); equal shares share a rank.

WHAT f -> INFINITY MEANS (read from the code before any result run; this is a derivation, not a result)
  Every place the sideways time enters is a necessary condition for a draw to succeed:
    sweep2.py:127   across = (L / U.A) ** 2 / Dh <= t
    sweep2.py:129   creep = across & ...            sweep2.py:137   reached = ok & np.where(column, creep, across)
    sweep3.py:122-123   t_sel = max(10/k, (L/U.A)**2/Dh, ...);  timely = t_rise + t_sel <= t
    multiwall.py:421-422   t_sel includes (L/U.A)**2/Dh;  timely = rec & (scale*up + t_sel <= t)
  and multi() returns w_f | (deep & strong) combined with the timely branch, so a draw whose sideways test fails has
  P_pved = 0 in all four columns.  A draw passes only if f * ratio <= 1 (in the timely branch 1 - t_rise/t), ratio =
  (pi R / 5.5)^2 / (D_h t), with D_h and t log-uniform over each world's declared range.  So for every world there is a
  finite f above which EVERY draw fails and the share is exactly 0:  Miranda 8.7e7, Umbriel 1.1e8, Ariel 1.15e8, Dione 1.2e8,
  Enceladus 1.8e8 (the last), all others lower.  The limit f -> infinity is therefore degenerate: all 15 worlds, all 4
  columns, share exactly 0.000, Early Earth 99.93 -> 0.00, and the rank is a 15-way tie.  It is implemented as very large f
  (1e9 and 1e12, both above 1.8e8); np.inf is not used because U.A would be 0.  The informative result is the f at which
  each world collapses, which is what the finite grid shows.

  h13h14_combined_predictions.txt, journals/new_analyses_h13h14_combined.md, sweep2.py / sweep3.py / multiwall.py at the

    h13h14_extreme_forecast.py   sha256 78b461cf8ee5cca36ddbac6cb376cd25e9258d7fee8c3cbf0a5ce2bb30b0b858
    h13h14_extreme_forecast.out  sha256 742420778d7fc3b4d8b51664239bda1722dd025fd3e75ea9790bf48e38548e86
  It runs no sweep.  Model: share(f) = base share x (1 - p_flip(f)) x lam, p_flip(f) = P(f * ratio > 1) computed in closed form

  sampling noise (Ganymede at 1000: 26.457% logged, 26.341% analytic).  lam was 0.99-1.00 in the main columns at f <= 1000
  (Mimas 0.95, Enceladus 1.03, Ganymede 0.9915, Early Earth 1.000) and is an EXTRAPOLATION beyond that: it is the main
  source of error in every band below.  Nothing at f > 1000 has been run; no band was fitted to a result.

 The measured or ceilinged range of the combined factor is f <= 221 (sphere 17 x molecular-only ceiling S 9-13); f above
 that is extrapolation of a power law and rests on no measurement.  All results hold for f applied to every draw (a worst
 case) and for the assumed product 17 x S.
 X1. Early Earth headline.  If P3 holds at f = 3000 and f = 1e4 (99.8, then about 94): the headline 99.9 is kept ONLY with the
     bound "for a slowing factor up to 1000, 4.5 times the ceiling 221; it prints 99.8 at 3000, about 94 at 1e4, 82 at 3e4,
     61 at 1e5 and 17 at 1e6".  Unconditional "stays at 99.9" is dropped.  If EE still prints 99.9 at 3000, the bound moves to
     3000 and the note says the analytic onset model was wrong.
 X2. Early Earth first.  If P5a holds: "Early Earth has the highest share" is kept with the bound "for f up to 3e5 at least";
     if EE loses rank 1 at or below 3e5, the bound is the largest grid f at which it still has rank 1.
 X3. ija_draft.md:789 ("never limits").  If P2 holds at f = 3000 (Ganymede, about 17 points): "never limits" cannot stand for
     any f beyond about 40 (h13h14_combined_cross.log); the sentence gives the measured/ceiling range and, as extrapolation,
     the loss of Ganymede at 3000, 1e4 and 3e4 and of Early Earth at 1e5 and 1e6.  If P2 names another world, the sentence
     names that world.
 X4. ija_draft.md:790 ("finishes in time").  The basis is that sideways coarsening takes at most 7% of the age in any draw
     (Ganymede, margin 14.4); this holds world by world only up to its own margin (Ganymede 14.4, Early Earth 2400,
     Miranda 1755, Enceladus 18.4 from the f = 1 flip block) and in the limit no draw of any world finishes (P1b).  The
     sentence is bounded by the combined f and the limit is stated as a hard gate (every share 0 above 1.8e8), not as a
     worst-case result.
 X5. ija_draft.md:791 ("the order holds by construction against any effect that hits every world equally").  Kept as a
     conditional statement; the sideways slowdown is NOT equal across worlds (it scales with L^2/D_h), and if P5b holds the
     top five do not change while the three largest moons fall from ranks 6, 7 and 12 to the lower half by f = 1e4 (P5c).
     In the limit the order is lost entirely (P1c): the sentence needs the qualification and the f above which it fails.
 X6. If a run fails or returns NaN at some f, the f and the exact error are reported and no later f is scored.

RISKS (named now).  (1) lam beyond f = 1000 is extrapolated: Ganymede and Callisto may exchange places in P2 at 3000 - 3e4
     (Ganymede leads by about 1.1 points at lam equal; a 2.5% change in one lam reverses it).  (2) Early Earth in the x10
     columns uses the timely branch (scale x rise + sideways <= age), which shifts the effective threshold; the x10 bands are
     wider.  (3) Counts near a threshold (Europa 10.1 at 3000, Miranda 0.9 or 2.8 at 1e4) can move by one.  (4) A numerical
     failure at 1e9 or 1e12 is not excluded; the extreme values were never run.

SCORING.  Each of P0 - P6 is marked HELD or FAILED as written, per f where a row is scored per f, with the log line numbers.
A band missed by any amount is FAILED; nothing is re-tuned after the run.  The summary of the note gives the count of items.
