IS aped.py's SWITCH TO MASS ACTION (reading A -> B after run 1) SUPPORTED BY THE PAPER'S TEXT?
  Yes in form, and for a reason aped.py could not see: the PNAS Methods Jacobian M1 (b=beta=gamma=0) has
  d[L*]/d[L] = a - x_l and d[L]/d[L] = -a - x_l - alpha*x_d, so activation is first-order mass action, rate = drive
  constant * [monomer] (the paper's drive is the constant a itself). Reading B (forward kA*x*[L], x constant) has
  that structure with a_eff = kA*x; reading A (forward rate independent of the drive, x only in the reverse step)
  has no counterpart in the 2004 text and is the wrong one. The docstring is honest that B was chosen after run 1
  because A cannot give an upper threshold (aped.py lines 30-33; aped.log L4-L5), so B holding P1/P2 is not an
  independent test; my reading of M1 is the independent support. Caveat: the 2004 model's activation is
  irreversible and its upper edge for b=0 is the existence limit, not an eigenvalue sign change, so B's
  reversible form (reverse kA*Kfx/Kact) is not from the 2004 paper either.
