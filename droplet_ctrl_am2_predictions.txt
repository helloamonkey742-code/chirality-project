DEFINITIONS (Part 1)
- n controls (one drop each), K ~ Binomial(n, p), p the TRUE misread rate, the same for every control.  The cap of prereg Section 2 item 1 / Section 6:
  NOT SCORABLE (NS) if K/n > 0.10, i.e. K > n/10.
- NS(p, n) = P(K > n/10).  Goal (1): NS(p, n) <= 0.05 at p = 6%, 8%, 10%.
- first(p) = smallest n in 128..1024 with NS(p, n) <= 0.05.  stable(p) = smallest n such that NS(p, n') <= 0.05 for EVERY n' in n..1024.  The cap is a
  sawtooth in n: n = 10m ... 10m+9 share the threshold K >= m+1, so NS rises through those ten values and drops at n = 10m+10.
- FS(p, n) = P(K <= n/10) = 1 - NS(p, n), "false SCORABLE": true p > 10% but measured <= 10%.  Goal (2): FS <= 0.10 at p = 11..14%.
- Cost of a control: the prereg states none (checked: no cost, time or money figure for controls in Sections 6-9).  Counts only: n extra drops on top
  of the 200 main-run drops (and up to 100 extension drops); 128 now, 16 per level over 8 levels.
- Combos tried in addition to the cap kept at 10%: caps of 6, 7, 8, 9% (K > cap * n), for the pair NS(6%) <= 0.05 AND FS(11%) <= 0.10.

PART 1 PREDICTIONS (n over 128..1024, every integer)
P1  p = 6%.  first = 128.  stable in [128, 150].  max over n of NS(6%, n) in [0.044, 0.056] (at the top of a plateau near n = 129).
P2  p = 8%.  first in [400, 520].  stable in [480, 640].  The sawtooth is visible: some n above first has NS > 0.05.
P3  p = 10%.  No n in 128..1024 has NS <= 0.05.  min over n of NS(10%, n) in [0.42, 0.50] (at n near 1020); max over n in [0.52, 0.60].
    NS at 10% never gets near 0.05 because the cap sits at the mean: P(K > np) tends to 1/2, not to 0.
P4  p = 11%, cap 10%.  No n in 128..1024 has FS <= 0.10.  FS(11%, 128) in [0.30, 0.37]; min over n of FS(11%, n) in [0.12, 0.19].
P5  p = 12%, 13%, 14% (cap 10%).  first n with FS <= 0.10: 12% in [420, 600]; 13% in [200, 330]; 14% at most 170 (FS(14%, 128) in [0.07, 0.13]).
P6  Answer to "does any cap / count combination keep false-SCORABLE under 10%":  NO for the cap kept at 10% when the true rate is 11% (P4); yes only
    for true rates >= about 12% with n >= about 500 (P5).  With a LOWER cap the pair NS(6%) <= 0.05 and FS(11%) <= 0.10 (stable over larger n) is met first
    at cap 8% or 9% with n in [330, 520]; a 6% or 7% cap never meets it within 1024 (6%: NS(6%) is about 0.5 at that cap; 7%: needs about 1500).
    Price of a lower cap: NS at a TRUE 8% (cap 8%) is about 0.5, so the 8-10% range described in prereg Section 3 becomes NS about half the time.

PART 2 SETUP (what droplet_ctrl_am2.py does)
- Cells: the Section 3 / Section 10 power tables are misread_sim.log's policy-B 128-control columns: 87 counting+drying cells (max STEP share per
  condition, log lines 10-21), probit s = 0.1, 0.15, 0.2, 0.3 (lines 31-42, 46-57, 61-72, 76-87) and probit s = 0.15 NOT SCORABLE (lines 91-102), 12
  conditions = CV {0.05, 0.15} x true misread {0, .02, .04, .06, .08, .10}, 500 runs per cell, seeds [5, i, j] (i over the 87 counting cells then the
  4 steps, j over the 12 conditions), controls drawn as misread_sim.run_cell does (rng.random((500, 192)) < eps, first 128 used, drawn after the runs).
- Rules, same simulated data for both: B = frozen (e_hat = (k+0.5)/129, c* = -2.21); AM2 = B plus a plateau h in both families, c* = -2.40
  (droplet_range_a.log:122-149).  Each reported WITHOUT the 10% control cap (what misread_sim.log and the prereg Section 3 / 10 percentages are) and WITH
  the cap (what Section 2 says).
- B must reproduce the log first.  c* is -2.21 here, the rounded value; misread_sim used its unrounded calibrated c*_B(128), so a run or two per cell can
  flip.

PART 2 PREDICTIONS
P7  B reproduction (no cap, 128 controls).  Of the 60 B cells (12 counting maxima + 48 step cells), at least 58 match the log to its printed digits;
    every cell within 0.015 (2-decimal tables) or 0.004 (3-decimal counting table).  B128 counting maximum over all 12 conditions: 0.054 (+-0.004).
    NS for probit s = 0.15 without cap: 0.00 in all 12 conditions.
P8  AM2 against B, probit steps, no cap, 48 cells.  At least 45 of 48 cells have |AM2 - B| <= 0.05; every cell has AM2 - B in [-0.06, +0.02].  The
    sharp steps (s = 0.1, 0.15) differ by <= 0.03 in every condition.  Any cell beyond 0.05 is an s = 0.3 or s = 0.2 cell at misread >= 6%.
P9  AM2, counting cells, no cap.  Maximum STEP share over the 87 cells is <= 0.07 in every one of the 12 conditions, and within 0.03 of B's
    maximum in the same condition.
P10 With the 10% cap (Section 2 as written), probit s = 0.15: NS share by true misread  0.06 -> 0.044 (+-0.03), 0.08 -> 0.224 (+-0.05),
    0.10 -> 0.519 (+-0.06), the same for B and AM2 within 0.02, and 0.00-0.01 at misread <= 0.04.
P11 With the cap, STEP share is at most (no-cap STEP share) x (1 - NS share) + 0.03, for every s and both rules: the runs the cap keeps have a measured
    rate at or below the cap, so the fit underestimates the true rate and calls fewer STEPs.  Example: s = 0.15, CV 0.05, misread 0.10: B with cap in [0.30, 0.50]
    against 0.92 without.
P12 The cap does not change the counting maxima (false STEP): with cap, B and AM2 maxima per condition <= the no-cap maxima + 0.01 (fewer runs survive, noise only).

DECISION RULES (fixed now)
R1 Gate.  If P7 fails (any B cell outside the tolerance, or fewer than 58 of 60 matching), the AM2 comparison is reported as NOT TRUSTED and no prereg
   wording is proposed from it.
R2 If the gate holds AND all 48 step cells have |AM2 - B| <= 0.05 AND the AM2 counting maximum is <= 0.07 in all 12 conditions:  AM2 can replace B without
   changing the Section 3 / Section 10 percentages by more than 0.05; the proposed wording says "AM2 power within 0.05 of B's" (NOT APPLIED).
R3 If the gate holds and any step cell has |AM2 - B| > 0.05, or any AM2 counting maximum exceeds 0.07: the Section 3 / Section 10 ranges must be restated
   from the AM2 table (the cells that exceed are listed), and the proposed wording uses the AM2 ranges (NOT APPLIED).
R4 Control count.  Report first and stable n at 6%, 8%, 10% as computed.  If no n <= 1024 reaches 0.05 at 10% (P3), say so; the proposed wording then
   states the NS probability at true 8% and 10% instead of promising a count.  Prefer n = multiple of 10 (cap threshold integral); a change of n needs
   c* recalibrated (B: -2.61 / -2.21 / -2.03 at 64 / 128 / 192, misread_sim.log:4-6), which this unit does not do.
