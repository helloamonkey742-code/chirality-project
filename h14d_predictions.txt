QUESTION (open item 14 / H14, journals/ija_draft.md:252; sibling analysis h14_eddy.log:81-171; previous step h14c_advect.log):
h14c measured the eddy slowdown S50 of wall clearing at l/w = 10 at D_mol/D_eff = 1/8, 1/32, 1/64: S50 = 5.76, 10.27, 13.21
(h14c_advect.log:83, :79, :77). The least-squares ratio exponent over the three is q = 0.40 [95% 0.33..0.50] (:107); the local
exponents are 0.42 [0.32..0.56] for 8 -> 32 and 0.36 [0.15..0.53] for 32 -> 64 (:115-116). A power law with q = 0.40 carried
to the real ratio ~1e9 gives S50 ~ 1.0e4 (h14c_advect_posthoc.log:35), far above the world break-even factors f_be = 100-10000.
That extrapolation holds only if the growth does not saturate. Two limits are known: a steady cellular flow gives S ~ ratio^0.5
(q -> 0.5); chaotic stirring gives a ratio-independent (at most logarithmic) S (q -> 0). h14d adds measured points at 1/16 and
1/128 and asks whether the local exponent is falling and whether S50 keeps growing past 1/64.

    dropped, and if more than 5% of replicates are undefined the interval is called undefined.
  - Delta q = q(64 -> 128) - q(8 -> 64), paired over the same bootstrap replicates (the 1/64 cell is shared).
  - The 1/64 cell is POOLED: the 42 runs of h14c (V64_10#0 and #1, read from h14c_advect_raw.pkl, seeds 3121, 3122) plus the new runs of
    V64_10n below, same flow, same gamma, same hold. The old-only and the new-only cells are printed as sensitivities.
  - Regime diagnostics (not predictions): kappa for each flow; the Lyapunov exponent per kick of the tangent map of the random-phase
    alternating shear (an exact product of independent shear matrices [[1, kappa cos th],[0,1]] / [[1,0],[kappa cos th,1]],
    th uniform), because the phase is redrawn every kick; the number of kicks to clear (S50 t17 / Tk).

  GROWING    if q_lo > 0.3.

  Local slope: FALLING if the 95% upper bound of Delta q < 0; RISING if its lower bound > 0; UNRESOLVED otherwise.
  What each outcome means (wording for item 14, ija_draft.md:252, is proposed in the journal and NOT applied to ija_draft.md):
  - SATURATING. The growth of the l/w-10 slowdown with D_eff/D_mol is no longer a power law with q = 0.4 at 1/128: the 7-decade
    extrapolation S50 ~ 1.0e4 at 1e9 (h14c_advect_posthoc.log:35) is not supported, and any power law consistent with the 1/128 point has
    q <= q_hi, so S50(1e9) <= S50(128) x (1e9/128)^q_hi (printed). Item 14 is narrowed at l/w 10 to a bounded range with S50(1/128) as its
    measured anchor, still an extrapolation to 1e-9 and still limited to l/w <= 10 (l/w 30 measured only at 1/32, 8 runs). Worlds are
    called covered only if their f_be exceeds that bound; the others stay open. The chaotic-stirring picture (q -> 0) is favoured over
    the cellular one (q -> 0.5).
  - GROWING. The slope has not fallen below 0.3 at 1/128, so the power law is still the working description and its extrapolation
    (printed for q_lo, point, q_hi) stands as the estimate; item 14 stays OPEN with S50 growing as ratio^q over 1/8-1/128; no world with
