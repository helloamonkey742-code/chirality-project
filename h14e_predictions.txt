QUESTION (open item 14 / H14, journals/ija_draft.md:252; previous step h14d_advect.log, journals/new_analyses_h14d_advect.md):
h14d measured the eddy slowdown S50 of wall clearing at l/w = 10 on a five-point ladder, D_mol/D_eff = 1/8, 1/16, 1/32, 1/64, 1/128:
S50 = 5.76, 7.71, 10.27, 13.05, 15.28 (h14d_advect.log:96-108). The local ratio exponent fell: 0.42, 0.41, 0.34, 0.23 [95% 0.13..0.28]
(:112-115), Delta q = -0.17 [-0.32..-0.09] (:119), and the pre-registered label for q(64 -> 128) was UNDECIDED (:147; the upper bound 0.28
missed the SATURATING cut 0.2). A post hoc log law S50 = -1.77 + 3.52 ln r fits the five points with rms 0.19, a single power law (q 0.36)
with rms 0.48 (h14d_advect_posthoc.log:10-11; both post hoc). The two pictures agree at 1/128 and part at 1/256: a log law and a power
law that keeps falling give nearly the same S50 there, a power law held at q 0.36 or 0.40 gives a visibly larger one. h14e adds ONE cell,
V256_10 (1/256, l/w 10), and asks whether the slope keeps falling. It is the same model, the same flow family, the same per-case gamma rule
and the same statistics as h14d; the 1/64 (pooled 378 runs) and 1/128 cells are reloaded from h14d_advect_raw.pkl, NOT rerun.

DEFINITIONS (everything not listed here is exactly as h14d_predictions.txt, h14c_predictions.txt and h14b_advect_predictions.txt;

  - S50 / S80 = Kaplan-Meier median / 80th-percentile time of wall clearing after the END of the sweep, over runs walled at freeze-out,
    divided by t17(L, D_eff) = (L/5.5)^2 / D_eff; S_half = the time at which the mean wall measure of those runs falls to half its starting value
    (h14b_advect.half_time), also in t17 (printed, not predicted). Bootstrap: resample runs, 2000x, seed = sum of the
    character codes of the cell name ("V256_10"); the reloaded cells keep their h14d seeds. 95% interval = 2.5 / 97.5 percentiles, undefined
    replicates dropped, interval undefined if more than 5% of replicates are undefined.
  - V256_10 flow and gamma are built by the SAME recipe as V128_10 in h14d_advect.cases(): L = round(2 pi (l/w) sqrt(D_eff)) with l/w = 10,
    eddy l = L/(2 pi), Tk = round(2.159 l^2 / (8 (D_eff - 1)) / 0.2) 0.2, A = sqrt(8 (D_eff - 1) Tk), gamma = min(0.01, round(1/(5 Tk), 4)),
    D_mol = 1, h = 1, dt = 0.2, xorshift noise, Delta = 0.5, 1, 2 with 7 runs each per job, hold 60 t17, cadence max(2, round(t17/60/0.2) 0.2).
    The script asserts that the recipe applied to D_eff = 128 and seed 3301 reproduces h14d's V128_10#0 spec exactly.
  - Ratio exponent between two ratios r_a < r_b: q(a -> b) = ln(S50_b / S50_a) / ln(r_b / r_a), r = D_eff / D_mol. Paired by bootstrap
    replicate index (bs[:, 0] of each cell), as h14d. q(64 -> 256) is the least-squares slope of ln S50 on ln r over r = 64, 128, 256
    (H.fit_p), with its own 95% interval from the same replicates.
  - Delta q = q(128 -> 256) - q(64 -> 128), paired over the same replicates.
