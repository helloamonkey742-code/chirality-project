RULE R (as approved; the arm_e_r6.py letter rule, arm_e_r6.py:262).  Y if a seed in the pool is followed (Delta >= 2)
at e_need in all three settings, P if in some, N if in none.  Admissible evidence:
 (a) a model at measured or published rates with a seed test at a setting's concentration;
 (b) a deciding count.  A hand decided among N_d independent units cannot follow a seed below ~2/sqrt(N_d) (counting
     form, Delta = e0 sqrt(N_d); inherit.py's sweep factor (pi k tau)^(1/4)/sqrt(2) only raises the need when
     pi k tau >= 4).  Following at e_need therefore needs N_d >= (2/e_need)^2 = 1.2e23 (pond), 3.8e27 (lake),
     5.1e35 (ocean).  Any route whose decision is made by crystals, surfaces or aggregates, or by <= 1e23
     molecules, is N.
 (c) a seed that decays, is only passed through, or does not set the final hand (a field, a surface or a crystal
     population sets it) gives N.
 ? only if none of (a)-(c) can be applied.

RULE L (lenient; reported so the author can choose, not the approved rule).  P if a seeded excess of <= 1% grows and
sets the final hand, in the lab or in a model of the mechanism (the repo's grinding model viedma_sim.py / viedma_big.py
counts for both grinding rows); Y only with (a) at e_need; N if a seeded excess decays, is only passed through, or the
final hand is set by something other than the seed.  A racemic start with a random sign no longer counts.
Note: under L the arms do not change.  Arm E's excess decays at measured rates (arm_e_rates.py) and arm G's template
seed is lost (arm_g_r6.log:46), so both stay N.
