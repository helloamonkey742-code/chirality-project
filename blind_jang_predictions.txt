blind_jang_predictions.txt -- BLIND.  Pre-registered predictions for Jang, Coquerel, Seo, Kim & Park 2024, "Microfluidic
antisolvent crystallization for chiral symmetry breaking", Lab Chip 24, 5055-5064, doi:10.1039/D4LC00658E.  Written
2026-10-20, before any outcome of the paper was read.  The sha256 and the time are in blind_jang.py's docstring.

PROTOCOL.  As blind_units.py.  The predictor (Claude, an AI model) has not read the paper.  It saw one setup report from a
scout agent told to withhold every outcome.  A separate reader enters the outcomes in blind_jang.py, in a later commit,
without changing this file.  The author got the PDF from C. McKay on 2026-10-20.

SETUP (from the scout).  Aqueous NaClO3, saturated at Tsat = 35, 40 or 45 C and cooled to room temperature, is made into
droplets in a coaxial glass-capillary device.  The oil is dodecane with 5 vol% ethanol, the antisolvent, and SPAN 80.
Droplets are collected under dodecane in open dishes, 10-30 per dish, and left unstirred at about 25 C for 1 h.  There is
no seed, no chiral additive and no stirring.  Each crystal-bearing droplet is scored L only, D only, or mixed (both
hands), by polarized microscopy.  Crystals per droplet are NOT counted.  Figs 3c, 5a and 5b plot one marker per droplet
against diameter d_e (axis 0-1000 um), in rows L, L&D and D.  Figs 3d, 5c and 5d are L and D histograms in about 50 um
bins.  Droplets analysed: 379 (35 C), 618 (40 C), 276 (45 C).  Droplet size is set by orifice and flow rate.  The
dishes, devices and replicates are not stated.

WHAT THE MODEL SAYS.  A crystal-bearing droplet ends single-handed if all its independent units share one hand.  A unit is
a primary nucleus that forms before the first crystal's copies fill the droplet.  Units are coin flips, so with N units
P(single) = 2^(1-N).  At a fixed primary-nucleation rate per volume, N grows with V, so P_single falls with size.
Its breadth depends on the form:
   first nucleus + K extra ~ Poisson(mu), P = exp(-mu/2):          0.8 -> 0.2 over a factor 7.2 in V, h = 1.40
   all nuclei at once, N ~ Poisson(mu) given N >= 1:                factor 5.4, h = 1.64
   either, with nucleation set by ethanol through the surface (mu ~ V^(2/3)): h = 0.94 and 1.09
 Here h is the free-width exponent in P = 1/(1 + (V/Vk)^h), and h = 3 means 0.8 -> 0.2 over a factor 2.52 in V.  Every
 counting form gives h <= 1.64.  A step gives h at the fit's cap of 50.
 ASSUMPTION: within one Tsat series every droplet has the same nucleation rate per volume and the same capture time.
 Ethanol uptake depends on surface/volume and transit time, so this is rough.  Faster uptake in small droplets would
 make mu grow more slowly than V and broaden the fall.
 Tsat: a higher Tsat puts more NaClO3 in each droplet, so the supersaturation at 25 C is higher.  Primary nucleation then
 rises faster than growth, so more independent nuclei form per volume and V0 falls.
 No bias: L and D are equally likely, at every size.
 The model cannot give V0's value.

PREDICTIONS.  V = (pi/6) d_e^3 (1 nL = 1e6 um^3); "single" = L only or D only; "mixed" = both hands.
 (J1) Direction, per series (J1a 35 C, J1b 40 C, J1c 45 C).  Mixed droplets are larger than single ones (one-sided
      Mann-Whitney on d_e, p < 0.05).  A series is NOT SCORABLE with < 5 mixed or < 5 single droplets.
 (J2) No sharp critical size.  Fit P_single = 1/(1 + (V/Vk)^h) per series (per-droplet binomial likelihood, h in
      [0.1, 50]).  The prediction is h <= 3 in every scorable series.  A series is scorable if it has >= 10 mixed and
      >= 10 single droplets, and, among 50 um bins of d_e holding >= 10 droplets, one has P_single >= 0.6 and one has
      P_single <= 0.4.  NOT SCORABLE if no series qualifies.
 (J3) Chance hand.  Pooled over all three series, L-only vs D-only counts fit 50/50 (two-sided binomial p > 0.05).
 (J4) Hand does not depend on size.  Pooled, the d_e of L-only and D-only droplets do not differ (two-sided Mann-Whitney
      p > 0.05).
 (J5) Supersaturation lowers the volume scale.  Fit P_single = exp(-V/V0) per series (per-droplet binomial); a series
      with no mixed droplet has V0 = infinity.  The prediction is V0(35 C) > V0(40 C) > V0(45 C).  NOT SCORABLE if
      fewer than two series have a mixed droplet.
 Reported, not predicted: per series, the mixed share, P_single by 50 um bin, Vk, h and V0; the L / D split per series.
 Data rule: the reader enters each droplet's d_e from the markers of Figs 3c, 5a and 5b.  If L or D markers cannot be
 resolved, L and D come from the histogram bars at bin centres and mixed from the markers.  The script prints how the
 entered counts compare with 379 / 618 / 276.

READINGS FIXED IN ADVANCE.
 J1 fails: larger droplets are not mixed more often, so the number of deciding units does not grow with droplet size.
 J2 fails: a sharp critical size, not the chance build-up of independent nuclei, decides whether a droplet ends
   single-handed.  The counting rule's breadth is then wrong for this system, and the paper's critical-size picture
   (hinted, item ii) fits better.
 J3 or J4 fails: a bias (a seed or dust of one hand, say) steered the hands, or steered them by size.
 J5 fails: a higher supersaturation did not shrink the volume scale.  Either nucleation per volume is not the control, or
   faster growth shortened the capture time enough to cancel it.
 Every outcome is reported as found.
