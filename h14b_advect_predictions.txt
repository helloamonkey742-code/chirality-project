QUESTION (open item 14 / H14, journals/ija_draft.md:252; E86 :187; Section 2.3 :479; sibling analysis h14_eddy.log):
the only measurement of wall removal under explicit eddies is advect2d.py at l/w = 3.6 (one box, n = 1). The sibling
h14_eddy.log gives, per world, the uniform slowdown f of the sideways coarsening time (L/A)^2/D_h at which 5% of the
{l > w, P_pved} draws are lost ("break-even f"); the l/w of those draws reaches 4-9 (median) and 35-178 (p90) at U = 0.02
(h14_eddy.log:60-74). How much slower is wall removal at l/w = 10 and 30, at the same D_eff = 8?

DEFINITIONS
  Model = advect2d.py unchanged in physics: 2D Allen-Cahn field, lam = clip(-1 + gamma t, ., 1), gamma = 0.01,
  eps = 1e-4/area, k = 1, D_mol = 1, D_eff = 8, w = sqrt(D_eff/k) = 2.83; kicked random-phase alternating sine shear
  (cubic Lagrange kick, explicit Euler between kicks), amplitude A, period Tk, D_eddy = A^2/(8 Tk) = 7 exactly,
  kappa = A k_flow = 1.47 (the B arm of advect2d.py). Same-geometry scaling: eddy l = L/(2 pi n); l/w = 3.6, 10, 30 at
  n = 1 means L = 64, 178, 533 and Tk = 4, 31, 277.6 (Tk grows as l^2 at fixed D_eff and kappa). Because Tk is 31 and
  277.6 against a sweep of 200 time units, the flow acts only a few times (l/w = 10) or not at all (l/w = 30) before
  freeze-out: this is part of what is measured, not a defect.
  t17(L) = (L/5.5)^2 / D_eff, Eq. 17 with A = 5.5 (ija_draft.md:493): 16.9 / 131 / 1174 for L = 64 / 178 / 533.
  Walled run = not single-domain (all coarse cells one sign) at freeze-out (t_after = 0). T = first time (cadence
  max(2, t17/60)) a walled run is single-domain, right-censored at the end of its hold.
  SLOWDOWN: S50 = Kaplan-Meier median of T over walled runs / t17(L); S80 = KM 80th percentile / t17(L). This is the
  quantity advect2d.log:49-51 gives at l/w = 3.6: of the 29% walled runs, 14 / 25 / 50 / 80% were single by 10 / 20 / 40
  / 80 time units, so S50 ~ 40/16.9 = 2.4 and S80 ~ 80/16.9 = 4.7 (the "x4" of E86). S is the same object as the
  sibling's f (a multiplier of t17), so S can be compared with the break-even f directly.
  Uncensored cross-check: S_half = (time the mean wall length over ALL runs falls to half of its freeze-out value) / t17.
  Bootstrap = resample runs (2000x); "spread" = 16-84% and "bound" = 2.5 / 97.5% of the bootstrap.
  Exponent p: slope of ln S against ln(l/w) over the h = 1, n = 1 series (3.6, 10, 30), by bootstrap.
  Molecular reference: same box, no flow, D = D_mol = 1 (S_mol = T/t17 with D_eff/D_mol = 8 inside t17).
