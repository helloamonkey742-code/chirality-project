QUESTION. How far do the world shares move when the sweep's sideways coarsening term (L/A)^2/D_h is slowed by the
COMBINED sphere x eddy factor f?  Method: h13_sphere_shares.py f=1,17,34,100,136,300,1000 (U.A = 5.5/sqrt(f), same
quantity as coarsen_slow.run(f)); loss = share at f = 1 minus share at f, in percentage points of all N = 200,000 draws
(1 point = 2,000 draws), seed 0.  "Largest loss" = the line the script prints: max over the 15 worlds and the 4 columns
(main no lid, main final, x10 no lid, x10 final).  "Main columns" = main no lid and main final.

WHERE f COMES FROM (each row is a pre-registered reading of an existing log; none of it is rerun here)
  sphere alone           f_t = 17   (p90 of T1/tau_sw on a sphere, h13_sphere_score.log)
  S50 = 1.4-2.2 at l/w 3.6 (sets A-E; B 2.01)  f = 17 x 2.0 = 34   (h14b_advect.log:58-80, the only l/w measured at several L)
  S50 = 5.8-6.3 at l/w 10 (sets F, G; set H, a larger box, 3.4)  f = 17 x 6 ~ 100 (98-107; 57 for set H)
  S50 > 8 (censored) at l/w 30 f >= 136 = 17 x 8 (a LOWER bound on the combined factor at l/w 30; set I)
  molecular-only ceiling S 9-13: f = 153-221   (sets J, K: 9.33, 13.36)
  f = 300 and f = 1000 are stress points beyond anything measured (S = 17.6 and 58.8).
ASSUMPTIONS (stated, not tested here):
  (A1) The two factors MULTIPLY: f = 17 x S.  The sphere factor was measured without flow; S was measured in a flat
       periodic box with flow, as time since freeze-out over the no-flow t17(L) of the same box.  Multiplying assumes
       the eddy slowdown is the same on a sphere and that the sphere's wall geometry does not change it.
  (A2) 17 is a p90 and S50 is a median, so the product mixes two statistics.  S80 is censored (> 5.3 to > 16) in most
       sets, so the product with S80 would be larger; f = 136, 300, 1000 cover that side.
  (A3) The script applies f to EVERY draw (a worst case: draws with eddy size l <= w, about a third of Ganymede's
       P_pved draws at U = 1 m/s, h14_eddy.log:95-98, would get no slowdown in nature).

     the f = 17 line reproduces h13_sphere_shares.log:38 (0.16 points, Ganymede, main no lid) to within 0.01.

(P1) (a) Largest loss and the world, printed by the script ("largest loss against f = 1 is X points (World, column ...)"):
        f = 17    0.15 <= X <= 0.17     Ganymede
        f = 34    0.60 <= X <= 0.90     Ganymede      (central 0.72)
        f = 100   2.50 <= X <= 3.50     Ganymede      (central 2.7; 2.50 is the floor from the arithmetic above)
        f = 136   2.90 <= X <= 4.40     Ganymede      (central 3.3)
        f = 300   4.60 <= X <= 7.20     Ganymede      (central 5.6)
        f = 1000  10.40 <= X <= 13.50   Ganymede      (central 11; Callisto is within 0.5 point and may swap places,
                                                       which would make this part FAILED as written)
     The column named is "main no lid" or "main final" (Ganymede has no lid, so both are equal; the script prints
     the first).  X is non-decreasing in f.

(P2) (b) Worlds that lose >= 1.00 point in a main column at f = 100:  exactly {Ganymede, Titan, Callisto}.
     Europa (0.4-0.6 expected), Enceladus, Triton, Mimas, Pluto and the other eight lose < 1.00 in both main columns.
     At f = 34 NO world loses >= 1.00 point in any of the four columns.  (The x10 columns are reported at f = 100 but
     not scored: they carry about 0.6 of the main loss, so Titan and Callisto sit near 1 there.)

(P3) (c) Early Earth ocean stays at 99.9 at every f: the printed values in all four columns equal their f = 1 values
     (99.9 / 99.9 / 99.7 / 99.7) at f = 17, 34, 100, 136, 300 and 1000, and its loss is <= 0.01 point at every f
     (expected 0.000-0.002).

(P4) (d) Rank order.  Rank = descending order of the 15 worlds by the share in a column, from the unrounded shares the
     wrapper holds, f against f = 1.  At f = 17 and f = 34: no pair changes order in any column.
     At f = 100: the SAME order in both "final" columns (main final, x10 final), and in the two no-lid columns the only
     pairs that change are Dione with Ganymede and Dione with Callisto (Dione, 42.0 / 32.2 with no lid, passes the two
     large moons that lose 2.2-2.7 points from 44.1 / 43.5; Dione's own loss is 0.03).  In main no lid BOTH swaps occur
     (Ganymede 44.1 - 2.5 or more = 41.6 or less < Dione 42.0; Callisto 43.5 - 2.18 or more < 42.0).  The x10 no-lid swaps
     are not required individually.  Margins that could still flip an "unchanged" pair: Ganymede/Callisto (0.6 at f = 1),
     Callisto/Pluto (2.4 at f = 1) in the main columns; I put 85% on none of them flipping.

(P5) First f of the grid with any world >= 1.00 point (any of the four columns): f = 100  (34 gives < 1.00, 100 gives
     >= 1.00).  The largest loss therefore crosses 1 point between f = 34 and f = 100 (log-log interpolation: f ~ 40-45).

 All of this applies to f applied to every draw (A3) and to the assumed product (A1); neither is tested.
