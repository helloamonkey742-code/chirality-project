            feed  g : feed L:D = (1+g):(1-g) (f*g*C0/2 into the a-equation), steady response read at tau.
            seed ee0: at t = 0 both the monomer pool and the dimer pool carry ee0 (best case; a = ee0*x, b = ee0*y),

  Readout   monomer ee = a/x; dimer ee = b/y; "best readout" = the smaller e_min of the two at each grid point.
  Noise     linear-noise (chemical Langevin) variance of the odd (a, b) sector from the channel list, Sigma/Omega from
            J S + S J^T + D = 0 (stable case); accumulated noise of the unstable mode (case b). Counting noise only.
  Followed (literal) : Delta = mean/sd of the readout at tau >= 2 (P(correct sign) = Phi(Delta) >= 0.977). This is the existing rule.
  Followed (strict)  : literal AND the readout excess at tau reaches >= 1% ee (the scorecard.py:40 reading); for case (b) also the
                       nonlinear saturated ee >= 1% (arm_e_rates.simulate at the chosen point) and the linear growth from the
                       inherited excess reaches 1% within tau (log-form gate).
  e_min     smallest g (feed) or ee0 (seed) that is followed, minimised over the grid subset below.
  Operating guard (stable points only): f*tau >= 5 and -lambda_slow*tau >= 5, so the pool is at steady state by tau (avoids the
            static trap f -> 0, where dimers pile up and trivially keep a seed). Sensitivity: guard 1, 2, 3, 10.
  Subsets   U (unconstrained): the whole guarded grid. E (engaged): grid points with margin >= 0.5 * (max margin of that
            setting and case), i.e. where the network is as close to its bifurcation as the assumed grid permits.
  Letters   Y: e_min <= e_need in all three settings; P: in some; N: in none (scorecard.py:9). Reported separately for
            (a) and (b); literal and strict; feed and seed; subsets U and E. The EXISTING-RULE letter = literal, feed, subset U,
            best readout. The SCORECARD-COMMENT letter = strict.
