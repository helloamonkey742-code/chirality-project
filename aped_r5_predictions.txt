R5 RULE (aped.py, scorecard.py): sigma = s* a >= 10/tau, s* = 0.0063166 (aped.log L89), so a is needed at
  a_need = 10/(tau s*) = 5.02e-7 /s (pond, tau 100 yr), 5.02e-9 (lake, 1e4 yr), 5.02e-13 (ocean, 1e8 yr)  (aped.log L99-L101).
  YR = 3.156e7 s.  a is the apparent first-order rate at which the amino-acid pool passes through an NCA.

ESTIMATOR (fixed here)
  No source prints a rate for NCA formation from a free amino acid, so a is bracketed.
  Lower bound.  Each peptide bond made by an NCA used one NCA.  With product yields y_n (fraction, as printed):
      Leman (basis unstated, so the smaller reading):  f_min = y2/2 + 2 y3/3  (yield = share of residues in that product)
      Danger (yield "relative to activated agent", so per activated residue):  f_min = (y2 + 2 y3) [CAA] / [Ala total]
      a_lo = ln(1/(1 - f_min)) / t.   Products not quantified (printed "Nd") count as 0.
  The other reading of the Leman basis (yield = molecules per monomer, f = y2 + 2 y3) is a sensitivity only; it is at most 2x higher.
  Upper bound (COS route only).  NCA formation is one way the thiocarbamate is lost, so k_cyc <= k_loss = ln2 / t_half with
  t_half = 10 h (Leman note 8: in situ thiocarbamate, 25 mM, pD 8.6).  a_hi = k_loss = 1.93e-5 /s.  No upper bound is available
  for the other routes (a_hi = infinity).

LETTER PER SETTING: Y if a_lo >= a_need.  N if a_hi < a_need.  ? otherwise (the need lies inside the bracket or no upper bound).
  A lower bound that fails does not make an N.
ROW LETTER (primary route = Leman entry 1: free Phe + COS, no additive, 25 C, aqueous):
  Y if all three settings are Y; N if all three are N; P if all three are decided and mixed; ? if any setting is ?.
  Routes with an added oxidant, metal salt or 80 C (entries 5-9, Danger) are reported but cannot change the row letter: those doses
  and that temperature are not shown to be prebiotic.  They only show what the activation step can do.

PREDICTIONS
P1  No direct rate.  Neither Leman 2004 nor Danger 2006 prints a rate constant or half-life for NCA formation from a free amino
    acid; Leman gives end-point yields at one time per entry (and thiocarbamate half-lives 10 h in situ, ~20 h authentic),
    Danger gives end points and a CAA decomposition curve.  The open-access searches find no time course of NCA formation from a
    free amino acid with COS, cyanate or NOx at stated aqueous conditions.  Fails if a paper read here prints one.
P2  Basal COS, entry 1 (Phe 50 mM, COS ~400 mM nominal, final pH 8.7, 25 C, 56 h, dipeptide 6.8%):
    f_min = 0.034, a_lo in [1.63e-7, 1.80e-7] /s; ratio to a_need: pond 0.34, lake 34, ocean 3.4e5.
P3  a_hi = ln2/(10 h) is in [1.90e-5, 1.95e-5] /s, which is 38x the pond need.
P4  Basal-route letters (pond, lake, ocean) = (?, Y, Y).  The molecule-basis reading (a_lo = 3.5e-7) gives the same letters.
P5  Ferricyanide, entry 9 (Phe 50 mM, COS ~400 mM, K3Fe(CN)6 100 mM, pH 8.8, 20 min, dipeptide 36.3%, tripeptide Nd):
    a_lo in [1.60e-4, 1.75e-4] /s; letters (Y, Y, Y).
P6  Metal entries 5-8 (Pb2+ x3 incl. Pacific water, Cd2+; free Phe + COS): every one gives (Y, Y, Y); the slowest is entry 8 (CdCl2,
    16 h) with a_lo in [3.7e-6, 4.2e-6] /s.  Fails if any entry has pond ratio < 1.
P7  Cyanate route, Danger entry 3 (CAA 20 mM + Ala 100 mM, 80 C, 53 d; Ala2 12.7%, Ala3 1.9%): a_lo in [5.5e-9, 6.7e-9] /s; ratios
    pond 0.012, lake 1.2, ocean 1.2e4.  It is at 80 C, there is no upper bound, and no Ea; not used for the row letter.
P8  Row letter = ? (pond ?, lake Y, ocean Y at the lab COS dose).  The missing number is the activation rate in the pond, now
    bracketed at 1.7e-7 to 1.9e-5 /s against a need of 5.0e-7 /s.
P9  COS dose.  Volcanic gas has up to 0.09 mol% COS (Leman note 14) and pure COS dissolves to 20-30 mM (note 5, 25 mM used), so at
    1 atm of volcanic gas the dissolved COS is 9.2e-4 of the lab saturation.  If a is proportional to dissolved COS (not measured),
    the basal a_lo drops to 1.6e-10 /s: lake ratio < 1 (lake becomes ?), ocean ratio > 1 (ocean Y), pond ratio < 1e-3.
    Fails if the lake ratio is >= 1 or the ocean ratio is <= 1.
P10 Rate-ratio check of the model (exploratory, aped.py functions copied, x fixed at x* = 0.3852 M, kR = 0, other base rates kept,
    kA = a/x* with a/kH = 0.385, 3.85e-2, 3.85e-3, 3.85e-4, 3.85e-5, 3.85e-6): sigma/a stays inside [s*/3, 3 s*] at all six values,

DECISION RULE
  Letters per setting and the row letter follow the rules above, from P2-P4 and P8.  If P10 fails, the s* a mapping is not valid at
  real rate ratios, then the per-setting letters are reported "under the aped.py time rescaling" only and the proposed wording says
  so; the row stays ? either way.  Failed predictions are reported as failed.  scorecard.py, README.md and journals/ija_draft.md are
  not edited; wording goes in journals/new_analyses_aped_r5.md marked "not applied".
