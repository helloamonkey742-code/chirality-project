WHY A v3.  v2 assumed that lowering kA at a free fuel x lowers the activation rate.  It does not: under reading B the forward rate is
a = kA x, so at kA = 1e-3 the window just moves to x = 376.5 M, where a = kA x = 0.3765 /s, sigma_max = 2.5575e-3 /s (aped_r5_scan.log,
config A), the same as at kA = 1.  What controls sigma is a/kH, the reverse activation (kA Kfx/Kact = 1e-3 kA) being a small term at
large x.  v2 therefore never reached small a: its grid stops at x = 1e4 M, where kA = 1e-5 gives only a/kH = 0.1 (the +1.5e-3 /s at the
top edge of the grid is the grid limit, not a window).  The correct slice is sigma against a at a fixed large x (activation effectively
irreversible, as it is for a real NCA), which is the same coordinate the lower threshold x_c1 = 0.010457 M of aped.log L36 is a point on.
NOT BLIND: the x* slice (aped_r5.log "Model check") showed sigma(kR=0) = +5.15e-5 at a/kH = 3.85e-3 and -2.72e-5 at 3.85e-4, and
sigma(kR=1e-4) = -1.46e-4 at 3.85e-3, +5.42e-4 at 0.0385.  W3 and W2 are therefore partly known; W4-W6 are not.
