THE THREE POINTS AND WHAT THE SCRIPT DOES
(1) Is the failed gate P7 (droplet_ctrl_am2.log:163, 56 of 60) caused by -2.21 being a rounded c*?  Calibrate B at 128 controls exactly as misread_sim.py does: 87 counting+drying
    cells (droplet_range_common.null_cells) x 12 conditions (misread_sim.CONDS: CV 0.05/0.15 x true misread 0/.02/.04/.06/.08/.10), 1000 runs per cell, seeds [4, i, j], protocol
    droplet_range_common.protocol2(exact=True) (same random stream as droplet_sim2.protocol), 192 control draws per run after the runs (rng.random((1000, 192)) < eps, first 128
    used), e_hat = (k + 0.5)/(n_c + 1), eligible = scorable, range >= 0.4, z <= 1.645, at least 50 eligible runs, c* = minimum over the 1044 cells of the 5th percentile of L.
    Print c*_B(128) to 4 decimals and its cell.  Then rerun the 60-cell gate (87 counting cells + probit s = 0.1/0.15/0.2/0.3, 12 conditions, 500 runs, seeds [5, i, j], no control cap,
    through the unchanged droplet_range_common.run_batch with the patched controls of droplet_ctrl_am2.py) with the unrounded c* ("Bu") and, on the same simulated data, with -2.21 ("Bn",
    the control: it must give the 56 of 60 of the earlier unit).
(2) What c* do B and AM2 need at 480 controls (60 per level)?  The same 1044 cells and the same simulated runs (same seeds [4, i, j]); a second block of 480 control draws per run
    (rng.random((1000, 480)) < eps, drawn after the 192-block) gives e_hat = (k + 0.5)/481; B = fit groups c0, s0; AM2 = c0, cH, s0, sH (droplet_range_common.Fitter, plateau h in both
    families).  The same recipe at 128 controls gives c*_AM2(128) for comparison with the -2.40 that came from droplet_range_a.py (300 runs, 8 conditions, 87 + 27 floor cells).  Not
    done: evaluation of power or false STEP at 480 controls with these c* (no fresh-seed run), and the 10% cap.
(3) How often does each rule reject a SHARP step when the truth is smooth?  Bak et al. (2023) Fig. 2a, Tinc 90 C series (blind_units.BAK_TINC90 through exclusion_bak.bak): 11 levels, n = 3,
    6 or 10 at the seven P = 1 markers (not printed in the paper) and 12, 6, 6, 16 at the last four.  For each n in (3, 6, 10) and each misread e in (0, .02, .04, .06, .08, .10) (18 cells):
    smooth truth = the best COUNT curve (c0 grid) fitted to that series at that n and e; sharp truth = the best STEP curve with s <= 0.15 (s0 grid) fitted the same way.  Simulate k ~
    Binomial(n, e + (1 - 2e) P_true) per level, 500 runs per cell and truth, fresh seeds [31, n index, e index, truth index], refit each run with the misread rate e known (e_fit = e, as in
    bak_policy_b.py; no control noise).  sharp = 2 (best ll over the rule's groups - best ll among the rule's STEP curves with s <= 0.15), exactly as droplet_ctrl_am2.py stage 3; B uses c0, s0
    (STEP curves: s0), AM2 uses c0, cH, s0, sH (STEP curves: s0 and sH).  Rejection = sharp > 3.84.  Every run counts, scorable or not (stage 3 also ignored scorability).  The same simulated
    data is scored under both rules.  Paired number: the same share when the truth is the sharp step, i.e. how often a sharp step that IS true is rejected (my reading of "sharp-truth
    detection rate": a rule that rejects everything would look good on the smooth truth and show a large number here).
